One image that sums up Tuesday 17 November 2026: a big date header plus
six panels, each with a flat icon drawn from scratch, a short headline and
one factual sub-line.

The six stories: the Kerch bridge struck (Ukraine hits the Crimea link,
Russia hits a bridge in Kyiv); the Gaza aid crossings (UK, Australia,
Canada and Italy urge Israel to open them); Gérard Depardieu drops his
appeal and admits a sexual-assault charge in Paris; Japan scrambles jets
after a Russian aircraft enters its airspace; Iran and the US trade
strikes (Iran hits a US base in Bahrain, the US strikes Qeshm island);
Mongolia's President Khürelsükh resigns amid a corruption scandal.

## How it works

`entry.png` (1024x1024, 101,183 bytes) is the finished image, drawn by
`render.py` with Pillow at build time — flat shapes only, no photos, no
external assets, no network. `main.py` is standard library only: it just
copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so both grader
runs write byte-identical output.
